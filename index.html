KC Usman

Entrepreneur · International Sales Professional · Digital Marketing Specialist

KC Usman is a Pakistani professional whose experience includes international sales, United States telecom sales, customer service, digital marketing, and business operations. He is the former founder and CEO of UA Tech Solutions, which he operated for approximately three years.

His professional interests also include web development, Python programming, and artificial intelligence. He is currently learning and developing skills in Python and AI.
